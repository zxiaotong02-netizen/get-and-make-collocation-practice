Get & Make 搭配及易混词专项练习

提示：本练习考察重点为 get/make 固定搭配，以及 break/work/want/speak/talk/say/tell 等易混词汇。所有题目均为全新语境，填空题请根据中文提示填入正确短语（注意时态，大小写不限）。

第一部分：单选题（每题只有一个正确答案）

1. The bus was so crowded that it took me ten minutes to ______.
2. We took a wrong turn in the forest and ______ completely.
3. The meeting ______ because the manager's flight was delayed.
4. I really hope my new roommate and I can ______ well.
5. Don't let the dog play in the kitchen, or it will ______.
6. It's normal to ______ when you are learning a new language.
7. The teacher asked us to ______ the truth about what happened.
8. Please ______ louder so everyone in the back can hear you.
9. My parents don't ______ me ______ out late at night.
10. The car's wheel ______, so we couldn't drive it to the garage.

第二部分：填空题（共10题）

注意：请根据括号内的中文提示填入正确短语，注意时态和单复数（答案不区分大小写）。

1. Please remember to (下车) the train at the next station.
2. Bring a map with you so you don't (迷路) in the big city.
3. Sorry for being late, I (被耽误) by a traffic jam.
4. She is a difficult person, but I have to (和某人相处) her at work.
5. I need to (打电话) to the bank to check my account.
6. Let's (制定计划) for our trip next month.
7. The boss (想要我们完成) the report by Friday. (提示：使用 want sb to do sth 结构)
8. My computer (坏了) yesterday, so I couldn't finish my homework. (提示：使用 be broken)
9. How many languages can you (说) fluently?
10. I like to (聊天) with my friends in my free time. (提示：talk with/to)

第一部分：单选题

1. A (get off 下车)

2. B (get lost 迷路)

3. C (get held up 被耽误)

4. C (get on with 和某人相处的好)

5. B (make a mess 弄乱/捣乱)

6. D (make a mistake 犯错)

7. C (tell the truth 说出真相，固定搭配)

8. B (speak louder 大声说话，speak 强调发声/说话的动作)

9. A (want sb to do sth 想要某人做某事，排除 make 后接不带 to 的不定式 B/D 也错，因为句意强调意愿而非强迫)

10. A (doesn't work 不工作/坏了，主语是单数 wheel，用三单否定)

第二部分：填空题

1. get off (下车)

2. get lost (迷路)

3. got held up (或 was held up，被耽误)

4. get on with (和某人相处)

5. make a call (打电话)

6. make plans (制定计划)

7. wants us to finish (或 wants us to do；注意主语 The boss 是三单，用 wants)

8. was broken (或 is broken；根据 yesterday 判断用过去时更佳)

9. speak (说某种语言)

10. talk (聊天)
